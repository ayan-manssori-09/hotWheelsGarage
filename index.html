THE MISSION

Preserving Automotive Art in 1:64 Scale

What started with a single '69 Dodge Charger has evolved into a lifelong pursuit of automotive history. My mission is to document and preserve the intricate craftsmanship of die-cast engineering through this digital museum.

From hunting in brick-and-mortar stores to trading with global collectors, every car in this garage tells a story of speed, design, and the thrill of the chase.
